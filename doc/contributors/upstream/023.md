# Lövetest's arc and setWireframe images assume one driver's rasterization

At the pinned LÖVE commit, `b7daef0f7`. Written as the report we would file; LÖVE accepts none made with generative AI (AGENTS.md, "Reporting upstream"), so it stays here.

Two of Lövetest's graphics tests compare what the GPU drew with a stored image at zero tolerance, though the geometry they draw sits where drivers may round either way. On real hardware they fail with stock LÖVE.

- `love.test.graphics.arc` (`testing/tests/graphics.lua`, lines 1329 to 1379) draws arcs of radius 16 with 10 segments on a 32 by 32 canvas and compares each with `testing/output/expected/love.test.graphics.arc-N.png`.
- `love.test.graphics.setWireframe` (lines 2508 to 2530) fills a 13 by 13 rectangle at 2,2 on a 16 by 16 canvas with wireframe on, and compares it with `love.test.graphics.setWireframe-1.png`. Under OpenGL, `setWireframe` is `glPolygonMode(GL_FRONT_AND_BACK, GL_LINE)` (`src/modules/graphics/opengl/Graphics.cpp`, line 1561); under Vulkan, `VK_POLYGON_MODE_LINE` (`src/modules/graphics/vulkan/Vulkan.cpp`, line 727).

The rectangle's edges lie on whole pixel coordinates, between two rows of pixel centers, and an arc's 10 segments pass close to pixel corners. Where a line or a triangle's edge passes exactly between pixels, the specifications let each driver pick the pixel it lights, so the stored images record the choices of the driver they were made on. `compareImg` (`testing/classes/TestMethod.lua`, lines 272 to 330) starts every test at an RGBA and pixel tolerance of 0 (lines 23 and 24). Only GitHub's macOS runners get an exception, with `--isRunner`: a pixel of tolerance for `setWireframe`, and `arc` skipped (`testing/readme.md`, "Runner Exceptions", lines 131 to 143). The readme says these tests "pass fine at the default 0 tolerance" on real hardware, but on this one they do not.

## To reproduce

On a Framework 13 with an AMD Radeon 890M (Ryzen AI 9 HX 370), Ubuntu 26.04, Mesa 26.0.8, with Vulkan's drivers hidden so that LÖVE takes OpenGL (renderer `AMD Radeon 890M Graphics (radeonsi, strix1, ACO, DRM 3.64, 7.0.0-43-generic)`), run the love built from `b7daef0f7` on Lövetest:

```sh
love testing/main.lua --method graphics arc
love testing/main.lua --method graphics setWireframe
```

`arc` fails 2 of its 3,072 checks, both in `arc-2`, the open arcs: at 2,7 the stored image is yellow and LÖVE draws white, and at 7,2 it is yellow and LÖVE draws black. `setWireframe` fails 49 of 256: the rectangle's top edge is drawn in row 2 where the stored image has it in row 1, and its bottom edge in row 15 where the image has row 14; the left, right, and diagonal edges match.

On the same machine both pass with Vulkan (RADV, `AMD Radeon 890M Graphics (RADV STRIX1)`), and with OpenGL on Mesa's llvmpipe under Xvfb. The same GPU passes under one driver and fails under another, so the stored images do not describe what LÖVE draws; they describe one driver's tie-breaks.

## A fix

Give both tests the tolerance the macOS runners already get, on every system: `test.pixel_tolerance = 1` for `setWireframe`, and for `arc` the same in place of the skip. A pixel's tolerance accepts a neighbor's color, which covers a line that lands one row over. Or draw away from the ambiguity: offset the rectangle and the arcs' centers by half a pixel, so that no edge lies between two pixel centers, and store new images.

## What Lota does

Lota matches LÖVE and leaves both tests as LÖVE has them. Its translations (`test/lovetest/tests/graphics.rb`, `test_arc` and `test_set_wireframe`) keep the macOS runner's exceptions and add none, and `LotaRunTest#test_lovetest_passes_when_run_by_lota_run` (`test/native/lota_run_test.rb`) runs LÖVE's own Lövetest in Lota unchanged. Lövetest offers no tolerance for this case, and loosening its comparison in Lota would hide a real difference from LÖVE.

Lota's CI draws with llvmpipe, where both tests pass. On the GPU above, Lota fails these two tests under radeonsi as stock LÖVE does, and passes them under RADV.
